Uncharted@IU Research Group
======
Welcome to the homepage of the **Uncharted@IU Research Group** in the Department of  [**Intelligent Systems Engineering**](https://engineering.indiana.edu/index.html)  at **Indiana University (IU)**. Our group explores **uncharted directions at the intersection of quantum computing and sustainable machine learning**. We aim to design **next-generation algorithms and systems** that are secure, efficient, and environmentally responsible—spanning both **theory and practice**, from hardware to applications. Uncharted@IU is led by **Dr. Lei Jiang**  [Email](email.png).

Research Focus
======
Our research centers on two tightly connected pillars:
### Quantum Computing
- Quantum algorithms and architectures  
- Secure and trustworthy quantum computation  
- Variational quantum algorithms (VQAs)  
- Quantum hardware–software co-design  

### Sustainable Machine Learning
- Energy- and resource-efficient ML systems  
- Sustainable and green AI  
- Responsible large-scale generative models  
- ML systems with long-term societal impact 

We are particularly interested in **bridging quantum computing and machine learning**, designing algorithms and hardware that are **both powerful and sustainable**.
